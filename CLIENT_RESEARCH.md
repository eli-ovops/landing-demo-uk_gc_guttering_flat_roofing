## Why manual commercial validation may be worthwhile

- A current paid local advert is evidence of ongoing local presentation spend; it is **not** proof the owner is shopping for a website.
- The service mix involves enquiry-led property work where clear service, area and proof information can reasonably support the initial phone/email route. That is a website-relevance inference, not a revenue or conversion claim.
- The owned site has authentic business material and public proof but does not put its core service choices or a clear direct contact path ahead of the fold on either tested viewport.

**Why now:** `NO CLEAR TRIGGER FOUND`. The 2026-08-06 advert is a current marketing signal, but no public source reviewed says the business is currently seeking a website, expanding, rebranding, or budgeting for one.
